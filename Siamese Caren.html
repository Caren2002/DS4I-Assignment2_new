Comparing the digits within people

The next thing we will explore is if there is variation within a persons handwriting. This particular section is important because later we will build a Siamese network that will compare whether two images were written by the same person or not. A preliminary analysis will visually help us determine if there are similarities within a persons handwriting.

Person 9

Comparing the digits of the same person

Finally, we will assess the within person variation of the different digits. The main goal of this is to assess whether there are similarities between a persons handwriting for different digits. This section is important because the Siamese and CNN networks will be tasked identifying similarities within the different digits.

For this section we will use the same three people from the previous section. We will keep the session constant and we will assess them on session one.

Person 1

Visually, person 1’s digits are centered on the image, and occupy similar proportions relative to the size of the images. Digits 1, 2, and 3 share a similar curved arc like top loop shape. Digits 1, and 2 have similar flat horizontal strokes at the base.

Person 9

Across individual digits, several structural and geometric shapes overlap: Digits 1, 2, and 3 share a smoothly curved top arch; Digits 1 and 2 both feature a flat horizontal stroke at the base; and Digit 0 and Digit 4 incorporate enclosed loop structures.

Person 18

Person 18’s handwriting samples exhibit strong slanted characteristics and angular features: Digits 1, 2, 3, and 4 all feature pronounced rightward slants and sharp, linear strokes rather than smooth curves. Additionally, horizontal stroke alignments appear across multiple numbers, Digits 1 and 2 share flat horizontal base lines, while Digits 1, 2, and 4 incorporate sharp, nearly horizontal top or middle cross-strokes.

Overall Comments

Visual analysis indicates noticeable variations in handwriting across different individuals. Even when writing the same digit, writers demonstrate distinct differences in shape, scale, stroke direction, and style. However, individual handwriting also displays consistent stylistic patterns; as shown in the previous section, a single writer often applies similar structural and graphical characteristics across different digits.
